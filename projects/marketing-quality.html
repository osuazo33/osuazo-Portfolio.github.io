Business problem

Marketing reports are only as good as the event data behind them. Missing identifiers, duplicated purchases or a silent tracking outage can make a campaign look better or worse than it is.

Objective: a documented product funnel and a set of quality checks that run before anyone reads conversion or revenue figures.

Data source

Google Analytics 4 public ecommerce sample: bigquery-public-data.ga4_obfuscated_sample_ecommerce.events_*, 92 daily tables from 1 November 2020 to 31 January 2021 with no missing day. Google obfuscates the sample: revenue is rounded to whole numbers and some traffic sources are masked as <Other> or (data deleted).

Every query filters on _TABLE_SUFFIX, reads only the columns it needs and was dry-run first; the largest scanned 0.27 GB. Two weeks are analysed: 1–7 Nov 2020, the first week of the sample, and 1–7 Dec 2020, a full week after add-to-cart tracking resumed on 26 Nov.

Funnel

A user reaches add to cart if it happens at or after their first product view, and purchase if it happens at or after that add to cart. A session is user_pseudo_id + ga_session_id.

Sessions: 7,807 → 2,563 → 414 (32.8%, 5.3%). Cart to purchase: 18.9% of users, 16.2% of sessions.

Tracking gap: add_to_cart has 0 rows from 2 to 15 Nov and from 21 to 24 Nov, while product views and purchases are recorded every day. In the first week 224 users purchased and 1 user has an add-to-cart event, so no November funnel is reported. Even in December, 103 of 537 purchasing users (19%) have no add-to-cart event, so cart to purchase is a lower bound.

What is not reported

ROAS and cost per acquisition need campaign spend, and this dataset has none. They are not calculated.

Data quality

A Python script runs four checks and logs the test name, run timestamp, affected rows, severity and resolution of each.

CheckSeverity1–7 Nov1–7 Dec
Missing user identifiersHighPass · 0 rowsPass · 0 rows
Duplicated transaction IDsHighFail · 3 IDsFail · 7 IDs
Unexpected event volumeMediumPassFail · 3 days
Stale dataMediumPassPass

Volume baseline: daily rows over 1–28 Nov 2020 excluding the days being checked, mean ± 3 SD (weekday 38,070–69,521, weekend 19,660–51,345 for the December run). The December failures (about 71,700 rows on 1, 3 and 7 Dec) are the seasonal peak, so a production version needs a rolling baseline. Stale data uses the latest table in the dataset (31 Jan 2021) as the expected date.
